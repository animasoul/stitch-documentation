# Why use Stitch Payments

Stitch Payments for WooCommerce accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover through CardPointe hosted fields. Card numbers stay off your WordPress server. The plugin is a standalone extension, so it works with your theme and with WooCommerce Blocks.

- **Checkout** — classic and Blocks checkout, guests paying with a new card, and saved cards for logged-in customers. See [Features](/docs/merchants/features).
- **Security** — a token instead of the card number, and no stored security code. See [Data and privacy](/docs/merchants/data-and-privacy).
- **Payments** — charge now or [authorize and capture later](/docs/merchants/authorize-and-capture), plus refunds, voids, subscription renewals, and pre-orders.
- **Your rate** — the processing commission is quoted for your store when you sign up. Stitch does not publish a single flat rate.
