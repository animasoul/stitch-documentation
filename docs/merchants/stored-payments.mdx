# Save cards in WooCommerce

Let returning customers pay faster by saving cards to their account.

## Enable saved cards

In **WooCommerce → Settings → Payments → Stitch**, under **Other settings**:

1. Enable **Enable Stored Payment Methods**.
2. Save changes.

Customers will see a **Save card for future purchases** checkbox when paying with a new card **while logged in**. The card is saved only when that box is checked. A note under the checkbox explains that Stitch Payments stores the card in a secure vault and that the security code is never saved. Edit that note under **Save card note** while stored payment methods are enabled. Allowed HTML is `strong`, `em`, `b`, `i`, and `br`. Guests can still pay with a new card; they will not see saved methods or the save checkbox.

## Customer experience

### At checkout

Logged-in customers with saved cards see a list of cards on the Stitch payment form. They can:

- Select a saved card, or
- Choose **Use a new card** to enter fresh details

Saved Stitch cards always appear inside the Stitch payment method — on both classic and Blocks checkout — rather than as separate top-level payment options. This keeps the [surcharge disclosure](/docs/merchants/surcharge) visible whenever a saved card is selected.

### My Account

Customers manage saved methods under **My Account → Payment methods**:

- View saved cards
- Add a new payment method
- Remove cards they no longer need. Removing a Stitch card also deletes that card's account at CardPointe. Other cards on the same profile stay stored.

**Add payment method** does not place an order. Stitch checks the new card with a $0 authorization, then saves the CardPointe profile on the customer's account. The security code is sent only with that check and is not stored. The customer must be logged in, and **Enable Stored Payment Methods** must be on. A declined card is not saved.

## Using Stitch alongside other card gateways

Stitch coexists with other payment gateways (WooPayments, Stripe, bank transfer, cash on delivery, and so on) without conflicts:

- **Checkout options** — every enabled gateway appears in the payment options list as usual. Only saved *Stitch* cards are moved inside the Stitch payment method; other gateways' saved cards still appear wherever those gateways display them (for example, as WooCommerce's top-level saved-card options on Blocks checkout).
- **Default selection** — if a customer's only saved card is a Stitch card, checkout preselects the first enabled gateway rather than silently preselecting the hidden card.
- **My Account** — **Payment methods** lists saved cards from all gateways, including Stitch.
- **No data crossover** — Stitch payment tokens, order metadata, and the surcharge are all scoped to Stitch. An order paid with another gateway never records a Stitch surcharge (see [Surcharge disclosure](/docs/merchants/surcharge)).

One shopper-experience note: running two card processors at once means customers see two "pay by card" options. Most merchants enable a single card gateway, or set distinct titles (for example "Credit card (Stitch)") to avoid confusion.

## Subscriptions

WooCommerce Subscriptions renewal orders require a reusable payment method. When subscriptions are enabled, saved tokens are used for scheduled renewals. Those renewals are sent to CardPointe as recurring charges. A pay-upon-release pre-order is still a one-time charge on the saved profile.

When the merchant account has Card Account Updater, saved profiles are enrolled. Once a day the plugin reads those updates and refreshes the expiry and last four on the saved card, or removes a card the brand reports as closed or invalid.

## Pre-Orders

_Since 0.5.4._ With [WooCommerce Pre-Orders](https://woocommerce.com/products/woocommerce-pre-orders/) active:

- **Charge upfront** — checkout charges the order total, the same as any other Stitch payment.
- **Pay upon release** — checkout does not charge the order total. A logged-in customer verifies a new card with a $0 authorization, or selects a card already saved on their account. When you release the pre-order, Stitch charges that saved profile for the order total. That release is a one-time charge. A subscription renewal is sent as a recurring charge.

Guests cannot complete a pay-upon-release pre-order; they are asked to log in. A release with no saved card, or a decline from CardPointe, marks the order failed and adds an order note. Address and CVV no-match actions apply to the $0 check at checkout. They do not apply to the release charge.

## Data and privacy

Saved cards use WooCommerce payment tokens with CardPointe profile and account identifiers — not full card numbers. CVV is never stored.

Each saved card records its brand (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), last four digits, and expiry for display. The brand comes from CardPointe's response when the card is saved; if the response omits it, the plugin derives it from the secure token (since 0.4.0), so the correct card logo appears at checkout and in My Account.

Before a saved method is charged, the plugin verifies that the profile and account identifiers belong to the paying customer's own saved tokens — one customer's stored card can never be used by another account.

See [Data and privacy](/docs/merchants/data-and-privacy) for tokenization, order metadata, logging, uninstall options, and privacy-policy guidance.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Check |
|-------|-------|
| Save checkbox missing | **Enable Stored Payment Methods** is on and the customer is logged in (guests never see the checkbox) |
| Saved cards not listed | Customer has tokens for gateway `stitch`; profile/acct IDs must be present |
| Guest cannot use a saved card | Expected — guests pay with a new card only. Saving and reusing cards requires an account |
