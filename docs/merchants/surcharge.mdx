# Credit card surcharge in WooCommerce

<MerchantSignup />

Stitch Payments can inform customers about credit card processing fees before they complete payment.

## Enable surcharge disclosure

Under **Other settings** in gateway configuration:

1. Enable **Enable Surcharge**.
2. Save changes.

![Other settings with Enable Surcharge turned on. Transaction type, commercial card data, and address checks are on the same screen.](/img/docs/admin-other-settings-rows.png)

The fee percentage is the rate boarded on the merchant account at CardPointe. The plugin uses the amount CardPointe returns. It does not have its own rate setting.

## What customers see

When a valid card is entered (or a saved card is selected), the checkout form can display:

- A calculating message while the fee is looked up
- A notice with the estimated processing fee, or
- A message that no fee applies to the card used

After CardPointe returns a surcharge for the card, that amount is added to the WooCommerce order as a **Credit card surcharge** fee line. The plugin does not calculate that amount from its own rate, and it is not carried over from an earlier checkout. Checkout shows the notice beside the card fields. The order total shows the fee line.

The settings preview shows where that notice sits. The **$1.50** line in this picture is a sample, so you can see the layout before you save. On the store, the amount comes back from CardPointe after the card is tokenized. The Visa ending 4242 is a sample saved card in the preview, not a live card on file.

![Dark checkout preview with a sample surcharge note under the card fields.](/img/docs/checkout-style-dark.png)

Fee lookups for saved cards only work for the logged-in customer's own saved methods — the lookup endpoint rejects profile identifiers that don't belong to the current customer.

## Order total and the amount sent to CardPointe

CardPointe applies the surcharge on top of the authorized purchase amount. The plugin adds that fee to the WooCommerce order so the order total matches what the customer pays, and leaves the fee out of the amount sent to CardPointe so it is not charged twice.

## Order records

The fee line is the surcharge on the order. The plugin also stores the amount in order meta for the payment log. After a test payment, **Stitch Payment Details** on the order shows the fee and states that CardPointe adds it on top of the authorized amount. The merchant ID is hidden in this picture.

![Stitch Payment Details on a test order, with a $0.60 credit card surcharge.](/img/docs/order-surcharge.png)

The disclosed fee is only ever recorded on orders **paid with Stitch**. If the customer calculates a fee and then switches to another payment method (for example a different card gateway or cash on delivery), the pending fee is cleared and the other gateway's order is unaffected.

## Testing

1. Enable surcharge and use the merchant account that is boarded for the fee.
2. Complete checkout with a test card in **Test Mode**.
3. Verify the notice appears and updates when switching between saved and new cards.
